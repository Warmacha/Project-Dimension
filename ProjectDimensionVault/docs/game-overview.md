# Game Overview

## Working Titles
- **Project Dimension** (current)
- "Train of Thought" (suggested, on-the-nose)
- "Runaway" (reference to runaway trains + the AI program running away with corruption)

## Hook

A game about finding tickets to travel to different worlds via an ever-present train. Each world possesses unique characteristics, interactions, and NPCs that slowly reveal an overarching story involving a dimension machine, corrupted AI programs, scientists, and the memories of an old man.

## Tone

NOT grotesque, but can include depictions of non-erotic nudity and blood. Benchmark: if it would be in *EarthBound* or *Yume Nikki*, it fits here.

## General Premise

Scientists created the **Dimension Engine** (tentative name) — a machine that generates worlds from a person's memories. These dimensions can take the form of ideas, life events, or structures embodying a theme.

The machine is currently running on the mind of **John Smith**, a middle-aged man on his deathbed. The scientists sent an exploration program into the engine to catalogue its worlds, but the machine is unstable: it attaches cognition elements onto anything that enters, including the program itself.

The player is that corrupted vessel — now taking the form of a childhood friend dressed in a memorable Halloween costume from long ago. As a corrupted vessel, the player travels across dimensions, sees strange sights, learns about the host, meets varied NPCs (many also corrupted vessels), and pieces together their purpose. Meanwhile, the scientist team attempts to communicate, adding a layer of meta-mystery.

## Main Character

**He's just a little guy.**

A corrupted exploration vessel that the Dimension Engine has made to resemble an old childhood friend specifically on the day of a particularly memorable Halloween.

- **Appearance (tentative):** 1930s newspaper boy. Suspender shorts, button-up white collared shirt. Couldn't find a hat.

## Demo Goals

5 worlds, a handful of NPCs, as many interactions as imaginable per world.

---

## Scope Revision — 5/25/2026

Scope reduced. The demo will focus on just a few rooms unraveling between **two playable characters**: the boy in the dream world and the scientist in the real world.

### Dual-Character Structure
- The game starts with the boy. The player explores and learns until a plot point triggers a switch to the scientist.
- Playing as the scientist builds context that shapes how to progress as the boy.
- The dream world is actively molded by what is re-learned in the real world.
- Puzzle philosophy: **Outer Wilds-style** — find things, build your own context, travel between perspectives to unlock progression.

### Narrative Layer
- While playing as the boy, the player *is* actually the scientist observing the boy.
- The scientist may have selective amnesia. The dream world is in danger; the boy is trying to save it while the scientist — who doesn't want to remember — is preparing for something darker (*Disco Elysium* style internal conflict).
- The scientist is uploading memories into the program to wipe them. Each upload helps the boy progress but also recreates the scientist's failures and blunders inside the machine.
- The machine turns the scientist's memories into a young boy resembling his childhood friend.
