# Worlds

Worlds don't need to be large — they can be a single room or a single building. Worlds can have multiple layers and connect to each other through secret routes found only through exploration.

The game separates worlds into **Major** and **Minor** categories (distinction TBD).

---

## The Beginning — Dark World

The game opens in a dark world on a bench under a single arched street lamp. The camera doesn't pan until the player walks right. The bench and lamp disappear as you walk; the world slowly fills in with color, nature, and a forest path.

The path leads up to an overgrown rural train platform at night, stars overhead. Either wait 60 seconds for the train to arrive, or sit on the bench to make it appear immediately. The player cannot leave the train platform once they enter.

## Train / Platform World

**The first proper area.** The goal is to introduce patience, interaction, experimentation, and non-linear thinking. Doubles as a potential demo.

### Layout

An infinitely looping train platform. Instead of tracks, there is dark water — trains skim the surface. The player can walk left or right infinitely. Stairs at the top lead to the opposite side (TOP TRACK / BOTTOM TRACK). BOTTOM TRACK is devoid of NPCs.

A train arrives every real-world minute. Without a **TICKET STUB**, the player is barred from boarding.

### Getting a TICKET STUB (3 methods + 1 alternate)

1. **The Orphan** — Stand beside them for ~30 seconds; they walk away into BOTTOM TRACK and vanish. A Ticket Stub is left at their original seat.
2. **The Waiting Man** — Return to him after visiting other screens; he progressively slouches and falls over, dropping a Stub. Alternatively, wait and watch him be refused boarding — he'll drop it as the train leaves.
3. **BOTTOM TRACK secret** — Stand on the boarding platform in BOTTOM TRACK just as a train passes; a Stub appears next to the player.
4. **Walk into the water** (alternate) — The screen darkens, the player sinks. They resurface on TOP TRACK with a **DAMAGED TICKET STUB** leading to an alternate version of the train.

### NPCs

**THE CLERK** — Sits in a ticket booth, head always obscured. Idle animation: shuffling papers endlessly.
- Returning with a Ticket Stub causes him to freeze, then the OPEN sign shuts off, and he stands to reveal *there is no head beneath his hat*.
- If the player gives him the Ticket Stub, he punches it (rendering it useless). Happens only once.

**THE ORPHAN** — A small child sitting on the platform edge, kicking their feet.
- Interacting produces no effect (just turns to look at the player).
- After 30 seconds of standing beside them, they walk to BOTTOM TRACK and vanish.
- If given a spare Ticket Stub, the train stops; The Orphan is refused boarding, then walks to BOTTOM TRACK as normal. No Stub is dropped.

**THE WAITING MAN** — Stares forlornly down the tunnel. Interacting makes a faint rustling noise.
- After visiting other screens, his posture degrades: STANDING → SLOUCHING → FALLING OVER. He drops a Ticket Stub.
- If the player waits rather than picking it up, The Waiting Man grabs it himself — the train arrives, he's refused, he drops it again, then walks into the water.
- If given a spare Ticket Stub early, same outcome: train arrives, he's refused, he drops the Stub and walks into the water.

**Common thread:** Patience is required. These NPCs cannot be saved or helped onto the train. Their journey is their own.

### Radiant Events (chance-based)

1. **Station Announcement** — Garbled audio plays (chance to cut off mid-clip into silence).
2. **Liquid Lag** — Player's water reflection lags behind; if the player stops or turns, the reflection walks offscreen.
3. **Ticket Punched** — Sound echoes; The Clerk's animation pauses briefly.
4. **Repositioning** — The Orphan moves a few paces and sits again. After 3 times, guarantees their Ticket Stub drop.
5. **Incorrect Announcement** — References a code to a nonexistent world. Entering it leads somewhere unexpected.
6. **Platform Without You** — On cell re-entry, the player character is invisible but footsteps still play (muffled/pitched down). Returns to normal after 5 seconds.
7. **Water Walker** — Footsteps appear on the water surface in BOTTOM TRACK. Entering the water after this grants **SOGGY BOOTS**.

### SOGGY BOOTS & The Wrecked Car

Equipping Soggy Boots makes footsteps squeaky and causes water to ripple underfoot. Walking on the water leads to **THE WRECKED CAR** — a train car sticking diagonally out of the water at an angle, barnacled and mossy (long submerged), lights flickering dimly. Ambient sounds from Platform World are dampened near it.

**Inside the Wrecked Car** (greenish underwater tint; interior is dry):

- **Ticket Pile** — A heap of punched Ticket Stubs. Interacting plays a faint punch noise.
- **Emergency Cord** — Pulling it flickers the lights; for a moment the car appears full of NPCs before returning to normal. The greenish tint also temporarily disappears.
- **DROWNED** — A half-submerged NPC slumped and dripping. Interacting triggers: on next entry to the Wrecked Car, DROWNED is gone. A suitcase floats outside the door containing the **CONDENSATION CHARM**.
  - If DROWNED is never interacted with, they remain forever.

**CONDENSATION CHARM effects:**
- Adds a faint mist trail to the player.
- Reflective surfaces show different things: the old man's reflection, twisted player versions, NPCs as monsters.
- Near The Clerk: glass fogs up and "1101" is written — but since glass is backwards, the actual code is **1011**, leading to a special area.
- Near The Orphan (15–20 seconds still): The Orphan looks directly at the player, nods, and walks into the water. Leaves a Ticket Stub if the player needs one.

## Body World

Like *Osmosis Jones*. Consider the liminal quality of a ribcage as an artistic space. Embodies the sickness that afflicts John Smith. A Medical Student/Nurse NPC struggles to keep the world alive and healthy — an internal drive born from The Man's mortality.

## Addiction World

Reachable via the Conductor sidequest (crashing the train with cigarettes). A mouse NPC in a corner says "Man, Addiction World is great! Gotta get back there when I can!" — funny because mice can't smoke, and foreshadows/recaps the Conductor arc.

## Aquarium World

Fish are hyper-detailed pixel PNGs that do not animate while swimming. Interacting gives a hyper-bitcrushed cry and sometimes a profound statement. Every interaction provides a "tap on glass" option.

Fishing in Aquarium World yields **philosophical fish** (need a pun name from JT) and life lessons. After finishing fishing, an enemy spawns and sends the player to **Prison World**.

## Prison World

Where the player is sent if caught by an entity (escapable at any time with the Train Whistle). On first appearance, seems like nothing to do. With the right knowledge or item, the player can escape into a secret world.

- After escaping once, the cell door stays permanently unlocked.
- Possible radiant event: a neighboring prisoner breaks free and lets the player out (could be a recurring NPC or could attack and escalate to a "Super Prison World").
- Possible escape methods: shrink to mouse size and walk through bars; Solid Snake ketchup packet laydown emote triggering a warden check.
